Description & Personal Notes

A thorough exploration of the principles behind reliable, scalable, and maintainable data systems. Chapters 5 through 7 on replication, partitioning, and transactions provide the clearest mental model I have encountered for reasoning about distributed state.

Key takeaway from Chapter 7: write skew is easy to miss under snapshot isolation because it involves disjoint rows that share a logical invariant. Revisit the section on serializable snapshot isolation (SSI) before designing the next ledger service.
